# Exploratery Data Analysis - 
Exploratery Data Analysis(EDA) involves using statistics and visualizations to analyze and identify trends in data sets. The primary intent of EDA is to determine whether a predictive model is a fesiable analytical tool for business chellenge or not. EDA helps data scientists gain an understanding of the data set beyond formal modeling or hypothesis testing.
