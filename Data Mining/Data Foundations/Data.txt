# Five C's in Data
1. clean - Clean Data means data that has no missing values, no inaccurate data, no out of range data, no typos error etc. Having 100%clean data can be difficult to achieve There can be missing or inaccurate value that identify while you perform analysis. Cleaning data is also subject to the law of diminishing returns means every more hour you spent on cleaning data looks less profitable then amount of energy and time you wested in.

2. Consistent - conisitent means no duplicacy. The data you or your orgnaization have should be cosistent. inconsistent data makes it more complex to clean and maintain hence requires more money. Consistency is strongly coupled with your data management and data geovenence policies.

3. Conformed - Conformed datam means data that fits in your problem boundries. For example, you have to analyze the decrement in performrne of college students result then you should have data of student's result of years(limitation is based on the year from when the decrement started) any data other then this can makes your analysis obscure.

4. Current - When it comes to data, current can be realtive. Stock-market data, needs to be real-time if you are using it trade stocks.

5. Comprehensive - Comprehensive data is data that spans all the required dimensions of your business case. The data should have sufficient depth. if you want to analyze your customer then you have to go back a year analyze the patterns of customers. Once you have collected the data review it and make sure you are not missing categories of data: columns, years, regios, etc.
