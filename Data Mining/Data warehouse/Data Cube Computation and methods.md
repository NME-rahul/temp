## Data Cube Computation

Data cube computation is a fundamental concept in data warehouse and multidimensional data analysis. It enables the users to efficently explore the analyze data from different angles or prepective. Data cubes faciliate more flexible and insightful querying and analysis comapred to traditional realational database. This multidimesional repersentation enhance the ability to gain valuable insigth from complex datasets, making it valuable tool in field such as businedd intelligence, analytics and data mining.
