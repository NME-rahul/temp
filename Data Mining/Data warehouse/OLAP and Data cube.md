## OLAP(Online Analytical Processing)

It is a technology that enables multi-dimensional analysis of business data. It provide iteneractive access to large amounts of data and supports complex calculations and data aggregation. OLAP is used to support business intelligence and decision-making processes.

## Data Cube

Grouping of data in a multidimensional matrix is called data cubes. The Data Cube pictorially shows how different attributes of data are arranged in the data model.
for example: we have to arrange the city wise population of country(India) then subdivide the country into states and each each into city(we can do further subdivision if want locality absed population).

Here The country India will repersents the Cube and z axis repersents will repersents the states, y-axis cities and x-axis population.

**Data Cube Classififcation**:

a. Multidimensional data cube: it basically helps in storing large ammounts of data by making use of multidimensional array. it increases its efficiency by keeping an index of each dimension.

b. Realtional data cube: it basically helps in storing large ammounts of data by making use of relational tables. Each relational table displays the dimesions of the data cube. It is slower compares to a multidimesional data cube.
