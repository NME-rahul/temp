# Geometry and Linear algebra

* The core of linear algebra is the relationship betwen vectors(points in space) and linear Transformtion.
* Vectors as arrows: a vector like $\begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix}$ is interpretd as instruction to mve 2 units right and 3 unit up from the origin or arrow whose head is pointing to the to the coordinates $(2, 3)$ in $x-y$ plane, similariy for high dimensional vectors.
* Matrics as Actions: a matrix $A$ isn't only the box of numbers but a function that morphs the space of vector multiplies to it. it can stretch, rotate, or squish the entire coordinate coordinate system.
