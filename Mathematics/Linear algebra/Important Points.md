* If a graph $\mathrm{G}$ is connected and then its complement may or may not be disconnected.
* If a graph $\mathrm{G}$ is disconnected then its complement must be connected.
  * In $\mathrm{G}$, either $\mathrm{\bar G}$ of $\mathrm{G}$ will be connected, i.e. if $\mathrm{G}$ is dissconeted then $\mathrm{\bar G}$ will be connected and if $\mathrm{\bar G}$ is disconnected then $\mathrm{G}$ will be connected.
