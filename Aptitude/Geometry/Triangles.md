### Area of any triangle: **Heron's Formula**
$\sqrt{S(S-a)(S-b)(S-c)}$
* Where S: Half of the perimeter; $\frac{a+b+c}{2}$
* $a,b,c$: Sides of traingle

#### **Area of Triangle**:
$1/2 * base * height$

## Isoceles Triangle
* It Has two sides equal.
* **Properties**:
  * It has two sides equal and angles to the base of equal side are equal.
* The line Perpendicular to the side $BC$ is bisector.

**Proof**
* If $\angle BAD = \angle DAC$ then the perpendicular $AD$ is bisetor to the line $BC$
* $Cos\theta_1 = \frac{AB}{AD}$ and $Cos\theta_2 = \frac{AC}{AD}$
* $AB = AC$ and $AD$ is common side.
  
$$AB.Cos\theta_1 = AC.Cos\theta_2$$
$$Cos\theta_1 = Cos\theta_2$$
$$\theta_1 = \theta_2$$
