## Types of Mapping cardinaity constraints

1. **One to One(1:1)**
2. **Many to One(N:1)**
3. **One to Many(1:M)**
4. **Many to Many(N:M)**

---

1. **One to One**

There is at most one relationship from one entity to another entity.

for example, One student have only one StudentID and one StudentId can belong to only one student. So the relationship mapping between Student and StudentID will be one-to-one.
